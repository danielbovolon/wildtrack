Wildtrack

A daily brief for people who make sound: one real seed (a word, a concept, a quotation or a photograph), a real photograph, a poem, something to listen to, an idea from another craft, and news from every discipline that meets audio.

Made in Bolzano, Italy. The sister of Talkback, the control room edition. In film sound, a wildtrack is sound recorded without picture, free to become anything. Seeds, photographs, quotations and poems are real, found material, credited to their sources. Nothing here is AI-generated imagery or invented quotation. The news summaries are researched with AI and link to their sources.
